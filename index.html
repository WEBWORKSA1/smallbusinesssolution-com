FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every calculator runs in your browser, needs no account, and your inputs stay on your device. The site is funded by advertising, sponsors, supporters and partner referrals.

How does "Get matched" work?

You describe what you need, your budget and timeline. We review it and introduce you to providers in our network who fit. You're never obligated to hire anyone.

Is this legal, tax or financial advice?

No. Our content is educational. Rules differ by country, state and province — confirm decisions with a licensed professional. See our disclaimer.

Can my company be listed as a provider?

Yes — apply through the provider network page. We review every provider before introductions.
