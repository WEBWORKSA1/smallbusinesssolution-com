Official rules (summary)

No purchase necessary to enter or win. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

  • Eligibility: open to individuals aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live) who own or co-own an operating business. Employees of sponsors and their immediate families are not eligible. Residents of jurisdictions where such contests are prohibited may not enter.
  • Entry period: each contest runs from the first to the last day of a calendar month (11:59 p.m. Eastern Time). One entry per business per month.
  • Selection: this is a skill-based contest; winners are chosen by judges using the published criteria, not by random draw. Where required by law (for example in Canada), prize winners may need to correctly answer a skill-testing question.
  • Prizes: prizes are as described on this page for the relevant month. No cash substitution except at the organizer's discretion. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  • Publicity: by entering, you agree that your business name, story and website may be published if you win, without further compensation.
  • Content: entries must be original, truthful and must not infringe anyone's rights. We may reject entries that are offensive or misleading.
  • Privacy: entry information is used to run the contest and as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Changes: the organizer may modify, suspend or cancel the contest if it cannot run as planned, and will post any change on this page.
