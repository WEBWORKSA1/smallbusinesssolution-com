How to use these tools well

Calculators are only as good as the numbers you put in. Pull real figures from your bank statements and accounting software rather than estimates where you can, and run three scenarios: a realistic case, a cautious case with 20% less revenue, and an optimistic case. Decisions that only work in the optimistic case are bets, not plans.

Use the tools together. Start with the startup cost estimator to size how much money you need, test repayments in the loan calculator, confirm the price you charge with the margin calculator, and then check the break-even point to see whether the plan is realistic for your market.

Every tool includes the formula it uses, so you can check the math or rebuild it in a spreadsheet. Results are estimates for planning only — not financial, tax or legal advice.
