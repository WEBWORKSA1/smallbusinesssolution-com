About us

Built for owners who'd rather be running their business

Small business owners make high-stakes decisions — take the loan, sign the lease, make the hire, set the price — usually alone, usually late at night, and usually without a finance team. SmallBusinessSolution.com exists to make those decisions clearer.

What we do

  • Free tools. Calculators and generators that run in your browser, show their formulas, and never require an account.
  • Plain-English guides. Step-by-step playbooks for starting, funding, pricing, marketing and growing.
  • Get Matched. A free service that introduces owners to vetted providers for funding, websites, marketing, bookkeeping, legal, insurance and more.
  • Community. The monthly Small Business Spotlight contest, a daily news digest and curated videos.

How we're funded

We keep everything free through display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, referral arrangements with providers in our network, and contributions from supporters. Advertisers and sponsors never change our calculators or editorial conclusions.

Editorial standards

  • Guides are written to be practical, accurate and up to date, and are reviewed periodically.
  • We show formulas and assumptions so you can check our work.
  • We link to official sources (government agencies and tax authorities) wherever rules apply.
  • Content is educational and general — not legal, tax or financial advice. See our disclaimer.
