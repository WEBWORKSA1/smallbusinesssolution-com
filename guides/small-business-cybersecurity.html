Cybersecurity basics for small businesses

Attackers target small businesses because they expect weaker defences. These ten steps block the most common attacks at little or no cost.

The 10-point checklist

  1. Turn on two-factor authentication for email, banking, payroll, your website host, domain registrar and social accounts. Email comes first — whoever controls your email can reset everything else.
  2. Use a password manager and unique passwords for every account.
  3. Back up important data automatically, keep at least one copy offline or separate from your main systems, and test that you can restore it.
  4. Update everything — operating systems, browsers, website software, plugins and routers. Turn on automatic updates where possible.
  5. Train for phishing. Teach your team to pause on urgent requests, unexpected attachments and login links. Real banks and suppliers won't mind you checking.
  6. Verify payment changes by phone. Invoice fraud — where a criminal impersonates a supplier and "updates" their bank details — is one of the costliest scams for small businesses. Always confirm using a number you already have.
  7. Limit admin access. Give people only the access they need, and remove it the day someone leaves.
  8. Secure Wi-Fi. Use strong encryption, change default router passwords, and keep a separate guest network.
  9. Protect customer data. Collect only what you need, store it in reputable systems, and know your local privacy-law obligations.
  10. Have a one-page incident plan. Who to call, how to lock accounts, how to contact your bank, and how to inform customers if needed.
Start today: enabling two-factor authentication on your main email account takes about five minutes and stops a large share of account-takeover attempts.

Consider cyber insurance

Cyber insurance can cover incident response, data recovery, business interruption and liability. Insurers increasingly require basics like two-factor authentication and backups before they'll offer cover — another reason to complete the checklist.
