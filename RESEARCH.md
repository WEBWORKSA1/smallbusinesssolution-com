## Features implemented as a result
- 9 interactive tools with formula explanations + result-to-lead CTAs
- 5-step "Get Matched" lead form with autosave and pre-fill (Lendio/Nav/UpCounsel/Thumbtack pattern)
- Provider-network signup (supply side, Clutch pattern)
- Stage-based "pick your problem" navigation (SCORE/ZenBusiness)
- Business health check scored quiz (Nav/BDC assessments)
- Monthly spotlight contest with sponsor slots (Inc. awards pattern)
- Supporter tiers (Ko-fi pattern), careers/contributor network, advertise packages
- Auto-updating news + videos (GitHub Action), tip of the day, deadline countdowns
