How to choose a business name

  1. Say it out loud. If people can't spell it after hearing it once, word-of-mouth suffers.
  2. Check trademarks first. Search your country's trademark database (for example USPTO, CIPO or the UK IPO) before you buy signage. A conflicting name can force an expensive rebrand.
  3. Check the domain and social handles. Matching handles make you easier to find.
  4. Leave room to grow. "Austin Cupcakes" is hard to stretch to catering in Dallas.
  5. Register it. Register the name with your state, province or company registry and, if it matters to your brand, consider a trademark filing.
