The break-even formula

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Price − Variable cost per unit). The part in brackets is the contribution margin: what each sale contributes toward fixed costs. Once fixed costs are covered, every additional sale's contribution margin becomes profit. To find the units needed for a profit target, add the target to fixed costs before dividing.

Three ways to lower your break-even

  1. Raise price. A small price increase often moves break-even more than a large cost cut, because it flows straight into contribution margin.
  2. Reduce variable cost per unit. Renegotiate supplier pricing, reduce waste, or change packaging and shipping.
  3. Cut or delay fixed costs. Shared space, month-to-month software, or outsourcing instead of hiring.
Divide break-even units by your working days. If you need 25 sales a day and you currently average 8, the plan needs a different price, cost structure or channel — not just more effort.
