How to estimate startup costs

Split costs into two groups. One-time costs happen before or at launch: registration, equipment, deposits, initial inventory, branding and your website. Monthly costs continue whether or not you have customers yet: rent, software, insurance, marketing and the minimum you need to pay yourself.

Most new businesses take months to reach steady revenue, so cover at least six months of monthly costs. Add a 10–20% buffer for the costs you forgot — there are always some.

Commonly forgotten costs

  • Payment-processing fees (often around 3% of card sales).
  • Security deposits and first-and-last month's rent.
  • Professional fees for contracts, permits and tax setup.
  • Sales tax registration and collection systems.
  • Your own living expenses while revenue ramps up.
