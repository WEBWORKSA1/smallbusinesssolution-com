What every invoice should include

  • A unique invoice number and the issue date.
  • Your business name and contact details, and the client's.
  • A clear description, quantity and rate for each item.
  • Taxes shown separately, with your tax registration number if required where you operate.
  • The due date and accepted payment methods.

Get paid faster

Invoice the same day the work is delivered, set short terms (7–14 days), offer card or bank-transfer links, and send a friendly reminder the day after the due date. Businesses that follow up consistently collect much faster than those that wait.
